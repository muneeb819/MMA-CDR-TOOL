## Fixture provenance

All phone fixtures use synthetic 555-01xx North American test numbers or clearly synthetic international-looking strings. The tests do not contact carrier/verification services. PDF, DOCX, XLSX, Parquet, image, nested ZIP, path-traversal ZIP and corrupt binary fixtures are small and deterministic; `tests/fixtures/generate_fixtures.py` recreates them.
